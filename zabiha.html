Our Story

BUILT ON
REAL FIRE

Zabiha Burger & Fries was born from a simple belief: halal food should never mean settling for less. Every patty is charbroiled over an open flame, every bun is toasted fresh, and every ingredient is zabiha-certified halal.

We're New York's late-night answer to the halal burger craving — serving Flushing and Brooklyn till 2AM, seven days a week. No compromises. Just real burgers, done right.
